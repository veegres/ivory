# Support Ivory

Ivory is free, open source and built in spare time. If it saves you a night of on-call or a pile of shell
scripts, you can help keep it going — and steer where it goes next.

<SupportTiers />

## Other ways to help

- **[Star Ivory on GitHub](https://github.com/veegres/ivory)** — it takes one click and helps other people find it.
- **[Report an issue or suggest a feature](https://github.com/veegres/ivory/issues)** — real-world clusters are the best roadmap.
- **[Contribute](/docs/community/contributing)** — code, docs or a new database plugin.
